Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data Eventique collects, why, and the choices you have. It is written in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"), the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011.

1. Data we collect

WhatFrom whomWhy
Name, mobile number, email, cityAll usersTo create your account, send login OTPs and booking updates
Event date, partner's name, budget, checklist, guest listCustomers (optional)To run the planner, countdown and invitation tools
Business name, category, prices, photos, bank details, documents such as GST, identity proof and licencesVendorsTo verify the business, show the profile and send payouts
Booking, payment and refund recordsCustomers and VendorsTo manage bookings, refunds, payouts and to meet tax and accounting laws
Chat messages, reviews and complaintsUsersTo let customers and vendors talk, and to resolve disputes
Approximate locationCustomers, only if you allow it in your browserTo show vendors in or near your city. It is used on your device to pick a city and is not stored on our servers.
Language, theme and similar preferencesAll usersSaved on your device to remember your settings

We do not collect or store your card number, UPI PIN or net-banking password. These are handled directly by our payment partner, Razorpay.

2. Consent

We process your personal data on the basis of the consent you give when you create an account, and for legitimate uses allowed by the DPDP Act, such as complying with law. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account or writing to our Grievance Officer. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing already done, and some records may have to be kept by law.

3. Who we share data with

  • The other side of your booking: when a Customer books, the Vendor sees the Customer's name, event details and the messages they exchange, so the service can be delivered.
  • Service providers that help us run Eventique: Razorpay (payments), MongoDB Atlas (database), Render (hosting), Cloudinary (photo and document storage), Google (sending OTP and notification emails, and translating the app's text) and UptimeRobot (uptime checks). They process data only to provide their service to us. Some of them may store data on servers outside India.
  • Authorities: when required by law, a court order or a lawful request from a government agency.

We do not sell your personal data.

4. How long we keep data

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal data within 90 days, except booking, payment and tax records, which we keep for up to 8 years as required by tax and accounting laws.

5. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you have the right to:

  • get a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how it is used;
  • correct, complete or update your data (most of it you can edit yourself in Profile);
  • have your data erased when it is no longer needed;
  • have your grievances addressed by our Grievance Officer, and then by the Data Protection Board of India;
  • nominate a person to exercise these rights if you die or become unable to.

To use any of these rights, write to majorproject.g11@gmail.com from your registered email.

6. Children

Eventique is meant for people aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect personal data of children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

We protect your data with HTTPS encryption, one-time passwords stored only as hashes, access controls by role, and limits on login attempts. No system is fully secure, so please keep your email account safe and never share your OTP.

8. Cookies and local storage

We do not use advertising cookies. The app stores your login session and preferences (such as language and theme) in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version, and we will tell you in the app or by email about important changes.
