Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy explains how cancellations and refunds work for bookings made on Eventique. The exact refund amount for your booking is always shown in the app before you confirm a cancellation.

1. How payment works

When you request a booking, you pay an advance (currently 20% of the total, including GST) online through Razorpay. The Vendor then confirms or declines. The balance is paid later through the app, as shown in your booking.

2. Refund rules

You can cancel a booking yourself in the app until the Vendor confirms it. After the Vendor confirms, write to us from the booking or by email and we will cancel it for you under the rules below.

SituationRefund
The Vendor declines your requestFull refund of the amount paid
You cancel before the Vendor confirmsFull refund of the amount paid
You want to cancel after the Vendor has confirmed, and you ask us within 72 hours of bookingFull refund of the amount paid
You want to cancel after the Vendor has confirmed, more than 72 hours after bookingThe amount paid is usually not refunded, because the Vendor has blocked the date for you. We look at each request and the reason given. Any balance not yet paid is not charged.
The Vendor cannot provide the service after confirming, or Eventique cancels the booking (for example, the Vendor is suspended)Full refund of the amount paid. We will also help you find another vendor.

3. How and when you get the money

  • Refunds go back to the same payment method you used (UPI, card, net banking or wallet).
  • We start the refund within 2 working days of the cancellation. Your bank usually credits it within 5–7 working days after that.
  • Payment gateway charges are not deducted from refunds that are due under the table above.

4. Problems with the service

If a Vendor did not provide what was agreed, raise a complaint from your booking in the app or write to us within 7 days of the event, with details and any photos. We will review it with the Vendor and, where the complaint is valid, arrange a partial or full refund or another fair resolution.

5. Vendor payouts

After an event is marked completed, the Vendor receives the amount paid minus Eventique's commission (currently 8%) in their registered bank account.
